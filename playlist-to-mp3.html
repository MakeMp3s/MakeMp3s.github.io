Playlist to MP3
Turn your playlists into files you keep

Make MP3s is a desktop app for Windows and Mac that works as a playlist converter and downloader. It turns your Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube and YouTube Music playlists into MP3 files, a whole playlist at once, ready for your computer, phone, USB drive, iPod or MP3 player.

Why keep your music as files?

Streaming is great for finding music, but your playlists live inside someone else's app. Songs get pulled, catalogs change and prices go up. Files stay on your drive until you delete them, and they play offline on whatever player you own.

How it works

  1. Open your playlist on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube or YouTube Music and copy its link.
  2. Paste the link into Make MP3s, choose a folder, and pick an export setting up to 320 kbps.
  3. Make MP3s reads your playlist, finds matching audio online and converts each song to an MP3 file with album art, tags and metadata.

Convert playlists from Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube

Make MP3s works as a Spotify to MP3 converter, and the same goes for Apple Music, YouTube and YouTube Music. Whichever service you use, the steps are the same. Copy the playlist link, paste it into the app and pick a folder.

Spotify playlist to MP3

Open the playlist in Spotify, click the three dots, choose Share, then Copy link to playlist. Make sure the playlist is set to public so Make MP3s can read it. Paste the link into Make MP3s and download the whole Spotify playlist as MP3 files in one go.

Apple Music playlist to MP3

Open the playlist in Apple Music, click the three dots, choose Share, then Copy Link. Make sure the playlist is shared publicly so the link works. Paste it into Make MP3s to convert the playlist to MP3 files.

YouTube Music playlist to MP3

Open the playlist in YouTube Music, choose Share, then Copy link. Check that the playlist's privacy is set to public or unlisted so the link opens for anyone. Paste it into Make MP3s and pick your export setting.

YouTube playlist to MP3

Open the playlist on YouTube, choose Share, then Copy. Set the playlist to public or unlisted first, since private playlists can't be opened from a link. Paste the link into Make MP3s to turn the videos into MP3 files.

What you get in each file

Every song is saved as an MP3 with its album art, tags and metadata, so it shows up properly in your music app or on your player.

  • Cover art can be turned on or off in Settings.
  • If a playlist includes music videos, Make MP3s can look for audio-only versions, so skits and intros don't interrupt your listening.
  • When a track has both versions, you can choose whether to prefer the explicit or the clean one.
  • You pick the folder, and you get a summary after each batch with success rates and any errors.

Which quality setting should you pick?

Make MP3s can export at 128, 192, 256 or 320 kbps. Here's roughly what a 4-minute song weighs at each setting.

128 kbpsQuick. About 3.8 MB.
192 kbpsStandard. About 5.8 MB.
256 kbpsHQ. About 7.7 MB.
320 kbpsHQ Max. About 9.6 MB, the highest setting MP3 offers.

A higher setting can't make a lower-quality source sound better, so the original audio still sets the ceiling. If you're not sure, 256 or 320 is a safe choice for most listening. Pick 128 or 192 when space on a small player matters more. You can change it any time, and every option is included in the free tier. Keep your music up to date with one click

Playlists keep growing, and the folder on your computer usually doesn't. With Sync Library, you add a playlist once, pick a folder, and click Sync whenever the playlist has new songs. Make MP3s only downloads what you don't already have.

  1. Open Sync Library in Make MP3s and click Add.
  2. Paste your playlist link and choose a folder. It can be on your computer, or on a plugged-in device like an SSD, phone or MP3 player.
  3. Click Sync to download the songs. Whenever the playlist grows, click Sync again to pick up the new ones.

Syncing to an iPhone, iPad or iPod? Point Sync Library at a folder on your computer, then add that folder to iTunes or Apple Music. You'll find the full Sync Library and iPhone walkthrough on our Learn More page.

Ways people use their MP3 files

Once a playlist is saved as MP3 files, it's yours to use anywhere.

  • Listen offline on a flight, a hike or anywhere with a weak signal.
  • Copy a playlist to a USB drive and play it in the car.
  • Load tracks into DJ software or a video editor.
  • Fill a small MP3 player for the gym or a run, without your phone.
  • Keep a backup of playlists you've spent years building.
  • Bring an old iPod or MP3 player back to life.
