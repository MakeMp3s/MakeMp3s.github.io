Getting started

What is Make MP3s?

A desktop app for Windows and Mac that turns your playlists into MP3 files with album art, tags and metadata. It's currently in Beta.

Which streaming services does Make MP3s work with?

Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube and YouTube Music playlists. You paste a playlist link and Make MP3s does the rest. See how it works.

How do I convert a Spotify playlist to MP3?

Open the playlist in Spotify, click the three dots, choose Share, then Copy link to playlist. Paste the link into Make MP3s, choose a folder and an export setting, and start the download. The whole Spotify playlist is downloaded as MP3 files. See the steps on how it works.

How do I convert an Apple Music or YouTube playlist to MP3?

It works the same way. In Apple Music, YouTube Music or YouTube, open the playlist, choose Share, then Copy link, and paste it into Make MP3s. Each service has its own steps on the Playlist to MP3 page.

Can I download a whole playlist at once?

Yes. Paste the playlist link and Make MP3s downloads every song in it as a batch. The free tier allows 10 downloads a day after your trial, and Premium has no daily limit.

Does my playlist need to be public?

Yes. Set the playlist to public before you copy its link (recommended), so Make MP3s can read it. On YouTube, unlisted playlists work too.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 10 or newer and Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer). Intel Macs and Linux aren't supported yet. We're working on wider compatibility.

How do I copy my playlist link?

Open the playlist in your streaming app or on the web, choose Share (or the "..." menu), and select Copy link. Then paste it into Make MP3s. Step-by-step help is on the Learn More page.

Is Make MP3s free?

You can try it free. Your trial gives you every feature for 24 hours or 100 songs, whichever comes first, with no card or email needed, and it's once per device. After that, the free tier allows 10 downloads a day (resetting at 12 am) and includes audio quality options, cover art, silence trimming, download stats and the explicit-content option. Premium adds unlimited downloads, Sync Library, Edit Music, Auto Volume and Square Crop Cover Art.

What quality are the files?

MP3 with export settings of 128, 192, 256 and 320 kbps. Final quality depends on the source audio. Here's a guide to picking a setting.

What is the difference between 128, 192, 256 and 320 kbps?

A higher number means a larger file and more detail, up to 320 kbps, the highest setting MP3 offers. A higher setting can't improve a lower-quality source. If you're not sure, 256 or 320 is a safe choice for most listening. See the quality guide.

Where are my songs saved?

You choose. Click Change under Download Location and pick any folder.

How do I update Make MP3s?

It checks for updates automatically. You can also check any time by clicking Check for Updates.

Devices and syncing

Can I listen to my playlists offline?

Yes. Once your playlist is saved as MP3 files, they play offline in any music app or on any player, with no streaming service needed.

How do I put my playlist on a USB drive or play it in my car?

Copy the MP3 files from your download folder onto a USB drive, then plug it into a car stereo or speaker that plays MP3 files from USB.

How do I get my music onto my phone or MP3 player?

  • On Android, drag and drop the files.
  • On iPhone, transfer them with iTunes, or with the Music app on a Mac.
  • For MP3 players, sync straight to a folder on the device.

What is Sync Library and how do I use it?

Sync Library is a Premium feature that keeps your music up to date. Click the Sync button next to Download, then click Add, paste a playlist link and choose a folder. Click Sync to download the songs. When new songs are added to the playlist later, click Sync again and only the missing ones are downloaded. See the full guide on Learn More.

How do I sync a playlist to my iPhone or iPad?

Use Make MP3s to save the playlist to a folder on your computer. In iTunes or Apple Music on Windows, choose File, then Add Folder to Library. On a Mac, use File, then Import in Apple Music. Then connect your device by USB and sync it. On a Mac, that part happens in Finder. Older iPods need iTunes. The Learn More page has the full walkthrough.

Troubleshooting

It won't work on my PC or Mac.

Make MP3s needs Windows 10 or newer or a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer). It doesn't run on Intel Macs or Linux yet, and we're working on wider support.

Windows shows a SmartScreen warning. Is that normal?

It can happen with newer apps, even signed ones. Click More info, then Run anyway. The Windows installer is code-signed with a certificate from Certum.

A song didn't match correctly.

Matching isn't perfect. Tell us which song and which playlist and we'll look into it, and we're working on improving matching. Email makemp3smusic@gmail.com or use the Feedback page.

Downloads aren't working.

This is usually a network problem on your computer, so check your connection first. In rare cases, YouTube changes something and the download component inside Make MP3s needs an update to keep up. Once the fix is available, Make MP3s detects the update automatically. You can also click Check for Updates in Settings any time. If downloads still don't work, email makemp3smusic@gmail.com.
